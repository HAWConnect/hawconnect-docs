Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy explains which personal data HAWConnect processes when you use the web app, the iOS and Android apps, the sign-in site or this website. HAWConnect is an independent, student-maintained project. It is not an official service of HAW Hamburg. \
For any privacy question, write to contact@hawconnect.de.

## 1\. Guest use

You can use HAWConnect without an account. In guest mode, imported calendars and preferences stay on your device or in your browser. \
Online features such as menus, events, news and the map need network requests. The receiving servers process technical connection data, including your IP address, to deliver the content. \
Uninstalling the app or clearing your browser storage removes the locally stored data.

## 2\. Hosting and server logs

The HAWConnect backend, the sign-in site and this website run on servers rented from Contabo GmbH (Munich, Germany). When you connect, the servers process your IP address, the time of the request, the requested address, and browser or app information, to deliver the content and keep the service secure. \
Server logs are kept only as long as needed for operation and security, and then deleted.

## 3\. Accounts and sign-in

To create an account, we process your name, your email address and a password. The password is stored only as a secure hash, never in readable form. \
We send a 6-digit code to confirm your email address; it is valid for one hour. Password-reset links are also valid for one hour. \
After you sign in, we store a session, which expires after 30 days without use and is renewed while you use HAWConnect. \
HAWConnect never asks for your myHAW password. Confirming your email address does not verify that you are enrolled at HAW Hamburg. \
You can delete your account at any time in Settings. This deletes your account data and synchronised data.

## 4\. Calendar imports

Imported calendars may contain event titles, descriptions, locations, dates, times and information about other people. \
For myHAW calendar links, the HAWConnect backend downloads the calendar and passes it to your app. This briefly processes the link and the calendar contents. The backend does not store them for this purpose and keeps them out of diagnostics. \
Private calendar links can contain access keys. Treat them like a password.

## 5\. Synchronisation

When you are signed in, you can synchronise data across devices. It is sent to the HAWConnect backend and stored with your account. This can include calendars, event details, calendar links, preferences and bookmarks. \
Synchronised data is stored encrypted. The backend can decrypt it to provide the service; it is not end-to-end encrypted. \
Guest data is not uploaded just because you create an account.

## 6\. Marketplace

When you use the marketplace, we process your listings, their photos, messages between users, and reports. Other users see your listings, but not your account name or email address. Contact details you add to a listing yourself, such as a contact name or email address, are visible to other users. \
If you contact a poster, your message is emailed to them and your email address is shared with them so they can reply to you directly. \
Listings run for a limited time. Listings and their photos are deleted 30 days after the listing's run ends, even if you closed it earlier. Messages are deleted after 30 days, reports after one year. \
We use reports to moderate content and prevent abuse.

## 7\. Email

We send emails for confirmation codes, password resets, important account notices and marketplace contact messages. Emails are sent through Microsoft 365 (Microsoft Ireland Operations Ltd., Ireland). Microsoft processes your email address and the content of the email for delivery. \
If you write to us, for example through the feedback option in Settings, we use your email address and message only to answer and delete them once the matter is settled.

## 8\. Analytics

The apps send anonymous usage events to Aptabase, for example which settings or features are used and when errors occur. The events contain no name, email address, account identifier or calendar content, and are not used to track individual people. \
We use them to find errors and improve HAWConnect.

## 9\. Map

The campus map loads map tiles from OpenFreeMap, which processes your IP address to deliver them. Map data is © OpenStreetMap contributors. \
Your location is used only on your device, only if you allow it, and is not sent to HAWConnect.

## 10\. Images

Images in announcements and marketplace photos are stored with Cloudflare R2 (Cloudflare, Inc., USA) and loaded from there.

## 11\. Storage on your device and cookies

HAWConnect uses app and browser storage for calendars, preferences and offline use. \
The web app and the sign-in site use cookies that are needed for sign-in. HAWConnect does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

## 12\. Transfers outside the EU

Cloudflare is based in the USA, and Microsoft may process data in the USA as well. Where data is transferred there, it is protected by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

## 13\. Purposes and legal bases

\- Providing the app, accounts, synchronisation, the marketplace and emails you ask for: Article 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of a contract). \
\- Server logs, security, abuse prevention, moderation and anonymous analytics: Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in a secure, working service). \
\- Answering your messages: Article 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR. \
\- Keeping data where the law requires it: Article 6(1)(c) GDPR. \
You do not have to create an account. Without one, account features and synchronisation are not available, but guest use is.

## 14\. Retention

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above. Account and synchronised data are deleted when you delete your account. Marketplace data follows the periods in section 6. Reports you made and moderation blocks are kept for their own periods after account deletion, without a link to your account; a block keeps only a hashed form of the email address until it expires. \
There is no automated decision-making or profiling.

## 15\. Your rights

You have the right to access, correction, deletion, restriction of processing and data portability, and to object to processing based on legitimate interests. Where processing is based on consent, you can withdraw it at any time; this does not affect earlier processing. \
To use your rights, write to contact@hawconnect.de. \
You can also complain to a data protection supervisory authority, for example the Hamburg Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (HmbBfDI).

## 16\. Changes

We update this policy when features or processing change. The current version is always available here.
