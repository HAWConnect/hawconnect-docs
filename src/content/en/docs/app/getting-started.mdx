# Getting started

Choose **Continue as guest** to use HAWConnect without an account. Your calendars and preferences then stay on this device.

To sync between devices, choose **Sign in or create account**. You sign in on `account.hawconnect.de` with a HAWConnect account: your email address and a password of its own. You do not need your myHAW password.

## Navigation

Use the main navigation for Home, Calendar, Map, Food and Settings. In Settings you choose German or English, the appearance and other preferences.
