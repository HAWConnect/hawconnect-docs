# Food and menus

Open Food, select a mensa and choose the menu date. Each dish shows the prices for students, staff and guests; the student price is highlighted. Set your dietary choices in the food preferences.

<AppScreenshots lang="en" screens={["food", "meal-detail", "cafes"]} />

Use the allergen and food-label settings, and open a dish for the information the source provides. Missing information means "unknown", not "free of allergens".

German and English dishes use the respective official source where available. If a translation is missing, the original text may appear.

An empty day may mean the mensa is closed or no menu is published yet. Check the displayed date before relying on a menu.

For binding information on diet and prices, ask the [Studierendenwerk Hamburg](https://www.stwhh.de/en/menu) or the staff on site.
