# Feedback

Something not working, or missing something? Let us know.

- **Report a bug:** describe the problem as an issue in the [app repository](https://github.com/hawconnect/hawconnect-app/issues). Mention your device or browser and what you did.
- **Suggest an idea:** feature requests are welcome as issues too.
- **Contribute:** see [Contribute](/en/docs/contribute/contribute) for how to help yourself.

Do not post private data in issues, such as confirmation codes, password-reset links, calendar export links or passwords.
