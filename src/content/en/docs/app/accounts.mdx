# Account and privacy

## Guest mode

In guest mode, calendars and preferences stay on this device.

## Signing in

To sync across devices, create a HAWConnect account on `account.hawconnect.de` with your name, an email address and a password. Confirm the email address with the 6-digit code from the email; the code is valid for 1 hour. If you forget your password, choose **Forgot password?** and set a new one with the link from the email.

HAWConnect never asks for your myHAW password, so pick a separate password. Signing in requires the account and email service to be available.

## Sync

When you sync while signed in, calendars and preferences are sent to the HAWConnect service and stored there encrypted.

## Signing out and deleting your account

Manage sign-in, sign-out and account deletion in Settings. Deleting an account is different from just signing out; read the confirmation before you continue.

The currently published policy and the operator contact are in the app under **About → Legal**, and here: [Privacy policy](/en/legal/privacy) and [Imprint](/en/legal/imprint).
