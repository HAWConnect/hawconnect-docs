# Frequently asked questions

## Do I need an account?

No. Choose "Continue as guest" to use HAWConnect without one. Your calendars and preferences then stay on your device.

## How do I sign in?

Choose **Sign in or create account** and sign in on `account.hawconnect.de` with your HAWConnect account: email address and password. A new account is confirmed with a 6-digit code from the email. You do not need your myHAW password. Signing in requires the account and email service to be available.

## Where is my data stored?

In guest mode, only on your device. If you sign in and sync, calendars and preferences are sent to the HAWConnect service and stored there encrypted. Details under [Account and privacy](/en/docs/app/accounts).

## How do I import my timetable?

See [Calendar imports](/en/docs/app/calendar). Never enter your university password.

## Where does the menu data come from?

From the Studierendenwerk Hamburg, for the Berliner Tor, Bergedorf and Finkenau mensas. See [Food](/en/docs/app/food).

## Is HAWConnect in the App Store or on Google Play?

Not yet. The web app already works in your browser: [app.hawconnect.de](https://app.hawconnect.de).

## Is HAWConnect an official HAW Hamburg app?

No. HAWConnect is an independent student project. For binding information, such as semester dates, check with HAW Hamburg.
