# Download

HAWConnect works today in your browser, on phone and computer. The iOS and Android apps are not available yet.

## Web app

Open [app.hawconnect.de](https://app.hawconnect.de) in a current browser. Or use the short link [hawconnect.de/en/get](/en/get), which takes you to the web app.

Without an account, your calendars and preferences stay in the browser on this device.

## App Store and Google Play

The App Store and Google Play apps are not available yet. When they are, the store badges in the download section of the [home page](/en#download) will link to the stores.

<Callout type="info">
This page does not confirm a store release. Whether and when a version appears depends on what is actually published.
</Callout>
