# Features

What HAWConnect offers for your day at HAW Hamburg.

<AppScreenshots lang="en" />

<Steps>
### Food

Menus from the Studierendenwerk Hamburg mensas at Berliner Tor, Bergedorf and Finkenau. Prices for students, staff and guests, with the student price highlighted; set allergens and food labels in the settings. More under [Food](/en/docs/app/food).

<AppScreenshots lang="en" screens={["meal-detail", "cafes"]} />

### Schedule

Your classes at a glance, built from the calendars you have imported.

### Calendar and semester dates

Import an `.ics` file or an export link from myHAW, check the preview and refresh calendars when needed. Semester dates are in the app too. More under [Calendar](/en/docs/app/calendar).

<AppScreenshots lang="en" screens={["calendar-import", "timetable-settings", "semester-dates"]} />

### Map

Search for a campus, an address or a building such as BT7. The map covers Berliner Tor, Bergedorf, Finkenau and Armgartstraße. Directions open in your maps app. More under [Map](/en/docs/app/maps).

### HAW events and news

Events and news from the HAW website, right in the app.

<AppScreenshots lang="en" screens={["haw-events", "haw-news"]} />

### Marketplace

Browse listings without an account. To create a listing, contact someone or report a listing, you need an account.

<AppScreenshots lang="en" screens={["marketplace", "marketplace-detail"]} />

### Clubs

Look through student clubs and initiatives at HAW Hamburg.

<AppScreenshots lang="en" screens={["campus-life", "clubs"]} />

### Quick links

Quick access to links such as myHAW, Moodle, email and the library catalogue.

<AppScreenshots lang="en" screens={["quick-links", "home-more"]} />

</Steps>
