# HAWConnect

HAWConnect is the campus app for HAW Hamburg: your schedule, the mensa menus, the campus map and more in one place. The app already works in your browser at [app.hawconnect.de](https://app.hawconnect.de).

<Callout type="info">
HAWConnect is an independent project maintained by students. The university does not run or endorse it.
</Callout>

## At a glance

- **Everything in one place:** import your timetable from an ICS file and explore Hamburg cafeteria menus.
- **On the go, too:** HAWConnect runs on the web, Android and iOS. Guest data stays in the current browser or on the current device.
- **Your data, your choice:** continue as a guest on this device, or sign in with your email address to sync calendars and preferences. See [Account and privacy](/en/docs/app/accounts).
